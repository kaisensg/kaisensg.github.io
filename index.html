Staff lines are in the polite register you'll actually hear. You don't need to produce keigo — you need to recognise it fast.

How to use these: read your lines aloud, then play the whole thing back with the staff lines only. The gap isn't your Japanese — it's the half-second it takes to parse ～でよろしいでしょうか under pressure.
